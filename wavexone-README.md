# WaveXOne Trading Platform (UAE)

> Digital Marketing & Web Development project for a UAE-based trading platform.

**Live Website:** [wavexone.com](https://wavexone.com)

---

## Overview

WaveXOne is a comprehensive trading platform developed and optimized for the UAE market. This project combines **digital marketing strategy**, **SEO optimization**, **lead generation**, and **full-stack web development** to deliver a conversion-focused trading platform experience.

---

## Role

**Digital Marketing & Development Lead**

---

## Key Responsibilities

- Developed and optimized the complete trading platform website with modern UI/UX
- Implemented responsive, mobile-first design for cross-device compatibility
- Executed SEO strategy targeting financial services and trading-related keywords in the UAE market
- Built lead generation funnels and conversion-focused marketing strategies
- Managed production deployment with Cloudflare CDN, SSL, and security enhancements
- Coordinated marketing campaigns aligned with platform launch and user acquisition goals
